3. Permissions Requested & Why They Are Essential

To deliver its core storage optimization and security features, ShrinkMaster Pro requests certain sensitive Android permissions. Each permission is used solely for the stated functionality:

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_*):
    Purpose: Essential for discovering space-consuming duplicate files, scanning cache leftovers, purging orphaned app residue folders, detecting low-light/blurry duplicate photos, and saving compressed video files to user-chosen directories. All operations occur strictly on your physical device.
  • Query Installed Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES / GET_PERMISSIONS):
    Purpose: Used exclusively by the local Malware & Security Shield. The engine inspects installed applications locally on the device to detect potential sideloaded adware, rogue overlay hijackers, and untrusted APK droppers.
    System Protection: System apps (such as Google Play Services and Samsung OneUI services) are protected by a permanent system whitelist. Package inspection happens purely in volatile memory—package inventories are never collected, transmitted, or logged.
  • Foreground Service & Wake Lock:
    Purpose: Ensures that long-running video transcoding or batch file deduplication does not get interrupted by the Android OS when the screen dims.
  • Vibration / Haptics (VIBRATE):
    Purpose: Delivers subtle tactile feedback during user actions such as completing a 1-Tap Speed Boost or confirming item deletion.

🔒 System Safety Shield: ShrinkMaster Pro explicitly whitelists Android core packages, Google services, and device manufacturer firmware (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, etc.). The application will never attempt to delete or destabilize operating system components.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data and device permissions at all times:

  • Permission Revocation: You may modify or revoke storage access at any time through Android Settings → Apps → ShrinkMaster Pro → Permissions.
  • App Removal: Uninstalling ShrinkMaster Pro immediately purges all associated local app state and cache from your device.
